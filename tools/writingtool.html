The Method

  1. One question at a time. Answer it, then move.
  2. Write rough first. The mess is not failure - it's the material.
  3. Be specific. Not "the room" - which room. Not "the glue" - what colour is it when wet, what does it smell like. Every abstraction gets challenged.
  4. The sentence is always yours. Reflect your words back as fragments. Correct or expand. Never let anyone write the sentence for you.
  5. Flag clichés yourself. If it sounds like a press release, say so. Rewrite it rough before handing it back.
  6. Once the thinking is clearly yours, stop redrafting. Lock it and move on.
